Redecora El Mundo: the free virtual land game on a real world map

Redecora El Mundo is a map and simulation game for browser and mobile. The planet is divided into virtual plots: you choose the ones you like, give them your name and a house, upgrade them so they produce credits and compete with other players. It is free and there is nothing to download.

▶ Play now

The game interface is available in Spanish, English, Portuguese, French, German and Italian.

How to play

1. Claim your first plot

Move and zoom the map, find any place (your street, your town, a city you like), tap a free plot and press “Claim this plot”. Pick the house you want and the plot is registered in your name. Once claimed, nobody else can take it from you.

2. Earn credits

  • Daily gift: the more days in a row you log in, the bigger the prize.
  • Daily roulette: one free spin every day.
  • Invite and earn: you and your friend both get credits.
  • Production: each plot generates credits hour by hour.

3. Upgrade and decorate

Level up your plots so they produce more and the house grows, or swap it for another whenever you like. There are houses of every kind: villas, apartments, office buildings, shops, stadiums, hospitals, churches and seasonal event houses.

4. Compete and chat

Check the ranking of who owns the most plots, chat with players in your area, form your town and become its president if you own the most plots.

A world map full of life

As you zoom into the streets you see cars, pedestrians, trains, planes, helicopters, traffic lights, animals, day and night and even rain. It is a real map, not a board: you can play in your own city.

Frequently asked questions

What is Redecora El Mundo?

A free virtual land game on a real map: you claim plots, give them your name and a house, upgrade them so they produce credits and compete in the ranking.

Is it free?

Yes. You can play for free in the browser or installed on your phone. There is a daily gift, a daily roulette and credit production without paying; you can also buy credits if you want, it is optional.

Is the land real?

No. They are virtual objects in a game and give no rights over real land.

Can I own land on the Moon or on Mars?

Yes, by travelling by rocket from the game.

Can someone take my plot away?

Once claimed, the plot is yours and nobody else can buy it.

How do I get credits?

With the daily gift, the roulette, by inviting friends, by collecting the production of your plots or by buying them.
