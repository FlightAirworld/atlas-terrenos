What it is, in a few lines

Redecora El Mundo is a free virtual land game set on a real world map. Each player claims plots, puts their name and a house on them, upgrades them so they produce credits and competes with other players in the ranking. It is played in the browser and can also be installed on a phone; nothing has to be downloaded.

Story angles

  • Your street, your house: anyone can claim a free virtual plot on their own street, town or city on the real map and put their name on it.
  • From Earth to the Moon, Mars… and Prehistory: the game includes rocket travel to other planets and to a world of dinosaurs, each with its own plots and ranking.
  • President of your town: the player with the most plots in a town becomes its president and can build the town hall.
  • Collectible cards: a collection of 10,000 unique cards that players can trade with each other.
  • A city full of life: cars, pedestrians, trains, planes, traffic lights and famous landmarks drawn on the map.

Key facts

  • Price: free. There is a daily gift, a daily roulette and credit production without paying; buying credits is optional.
  • Platforms: web browser and mobile (it can be installed as an app).
  • Map: a real map: you can claim plots on your own street, town or city.
  • Beyond Earth: rocket trips to the Moon, Mars and the rest of the planets, each with its own map and ranking.
  • Mini-games: trivia, scratch cards, card matching and bingo to win credits.
  • Languages: Spanish, English, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese and Chinese.
  • Important: the plots are virtual and give no rights over real land.
